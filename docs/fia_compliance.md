# FIA Technical Regulation Compliance Analysis

## Article 4.1 Minimum Mass Compliance
* **Regulated Strict Mode:** Complies by adding $44.512\text{ kg}$ of tungsten ballast at the monocoque floor tray.
* **Unrestricted Mode:** Operates at $753.488\text{ kg}$ for maximum theoretical performance benchmarking.

## Article 5 Power Unit Material Regulations
* **Al-Li MMC Engine Block:** Restricted in official FIA racing; fully functional in Unrestricted mode.
